THE REASON WE’RE HERE

The announcement is the beginning of the story

When a model tops a benchmark, we want to know what the score measures — and what that capability costs to use. When a company reports growth, we look for the business behind it. When a government announces AI investment, we follow the difference between a promise and something people can use.

Statvero connects those questions. A place to understand how AI is developing as an industry, through the numbers we can actually trace.
