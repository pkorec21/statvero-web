Current public website

The current public application does not set advertising or analytics cookies, use an analytics SDK, or store search history in local storage. Search, chart controls and selected comparisons use in-memory state and URL parameters. A shared URL may therefore include your chosen models, countries or filters.

Private studio storage

The private Postagent studio may save drafts and editing preferences in local storage on the authorized user’s device. When Supabase authentication is configured, its client persists a login session and refreshes it to maintain authorized access. Session lifetime and server-side revocation depend on the configured authentication service. These storage purposes are separate from advertising or visitor analytics.

Hosting technologies

Production hosting or security services may introduce additional necessary cookies or similar technologies. Their actual names, providers, purposes and lifetimes must be recorded after the production configuration is chosen. This development preview does not declare an unverified hosting-cookie inventory.

Consent choices

There is no consent banner for optional tracking in the current public application because no such tracking is integrated. Before adding technologies that require consent, they must remain blocked until a valid choice is made, with equally accessible acceptance and rejection and an easy way to withdraw the choice.

Your browser controls

You can inspect and remove cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Removing private-studio storage may sign you out or delete locally saved editing preferences and drafts. External links take you to services with their own policies.
