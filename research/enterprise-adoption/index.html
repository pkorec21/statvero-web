Population, method and interpretation

Percentage of enterprises using at least one AI technology. Selected non-financial business activities (NACE Rev. 2 C–J, L–N and 95.1). Enterprises with at least 10 persons employed. Not intensity of implementation, productivity impact or share of workers using AI.

The EU includes Slovakia and Czechia and is a regional aggregate, not another independent country. The shared observation year is selected automatically. Missing years are gaps; values are not interpolated. Source flags are retained in the survey explorer below.

Method: eurostat-enterprise-ai-size-v1 · Eurostat reuse policy; attribution and stated exceptions apply. Adoption is distinct from cost savings, productivity, job creation and job loss; those outcomes need separate evidence.
