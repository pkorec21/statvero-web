A gap is not zero

Missing observations stay missing. Lines connect recorded values; incomplete years are identified. Percentage-point changes and percentage changes keep their distinct units. Percentage change from a zero baseline is undefined.

A snapshot is not a release date

A catalogue observation records what was captured at that time. A change between two snapshots does not establish the exact time it took effect.

Geography describes coverage

Europe and the EU are different groupings. Multinational models can appear in more than one country or region. Country associations are not deployment locations or national AI-strength scores.
