Clear about the data

How the public publication handles data, attribution and corrections.

BrowsingStorageReuseCorrections

Public browsing

The public interface does not require an account. Model searches and comparison filters run in your browser. Shared URLs may contain your selected entities and filters; they do not contain private research drafts or API keys.

Site storage and services

The public application does not include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK. Pages request published datasets and visual assets to display the publication. Hosting services may process ordinary connection information such as IP addresses and request logs; production hosting and its retention settings must be documented before launch.

The private Postagent studio has separate authentication and session handling. Its unpublished research, source snapshots and editorial tools are outside the public data exports.

Data reuse and attribution

Source datasets retain their individual licences. Publication on Statvero does not grant a blanket licence to every dataset, company logo or third-party material. Consult the linked source and its reuse terms. Company and model marks identify the entities discussed.

Corrections and revisions

Observation dates describe the data period; snapshot dates describe when a source was captured. Revised exports keep version references where available. Material gaps, incompatible definitions and unavailable measurements are identified alongside the charts.
